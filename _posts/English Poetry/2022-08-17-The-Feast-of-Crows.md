They dine on marble floors while grain runs thin,<br>
And wipe their golden mouths upon our skin.<br>
To them, a nation is a ledger sheet,<br>
A pile of bones to pave a sterile street.<br>

<br>

We pay the iron tax of sweat and tears,<br>
To fund the monuments of all their fears.<br>
They build high walls to keep the truth outside,<br>
While the starving truth is buried deep inside.<br>

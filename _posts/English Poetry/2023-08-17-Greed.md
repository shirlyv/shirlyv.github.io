The ministers assemble in the grey,<br>
To carve the futures of the poor away.<br>
They speak in gentle whispers of the state,<br>
While locking every righteous prison gate.<br>

<br>

A decree is signed upon a velvet desk,<br>
Making our daily agony grotesque.<br>
They measure progress in the stocks they own,<br>
And grind the public spirit into stone.<br>

<br>

And when the hungry gather in the square,<br>
They name the cry for justice "wild despair."<br>
They deploy shields and banners of defense,<br>
To guard their hollow thrones of insolence.<br>

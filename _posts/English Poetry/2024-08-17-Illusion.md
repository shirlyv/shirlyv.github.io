They stage elections like a traveling show,<br>
With smiling puppets dancing heel and toe.<br>
They promise rivers where the fields are dry,<br>
And feed the starving masses with a lie.<br>

<br>

The ink is fresh upon the parchment scroll,<br>
Yet every signature forecloses soul.<br>
They tax the air we breathe, the light of sun,<br>
And count the empty shells after it's done.<br>

<br>

Behind the podium of polished oak,<br>
A tyrant laughs and calls the law a joke.<br>
The judges nod in synchronized disgrace,<br>
While justice is strangled in a public place.<br>

<br>

We built their palaces with calloused hands,<br>
We bled to feed the borders of their lands.<br>
Now every pathway leads to iron bars,<br>
Beneath a canopy of stolen stars.<br>

<br>

So let them write their histories in gold,<br>
And guard the stolen wealth that they have sold.<br>
For empires built on tyranny and pain,<br>
Must drown at last in their own crimson rain.<br>
